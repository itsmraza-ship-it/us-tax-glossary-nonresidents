# Quick Acronym Lookup

| Acronym | Stands for | One-line meaning |
|---|---|---|
| EIN | Employer Identification Number | Your company's US tax ID |
| ITIN | Individual Taxpayer Identification Number | Tax ID for individuals without an SSN |
| SSN | Social Security Number | US personal tax ID (citizens/residents) |
| LLC | Limited Liability Company | The entity most non-US founders form |
| DBA | Doing Business As | A registered alternate business name |
| ECI | Effectively Connected Income | US business income taxed at graduated rates |
| FDAP | Fixed, Determinable, Annual, Periodical | Passive US-source income (30% withholding default) |
| BOI | Beneficial Ownership Information | Reporting requirement removed for US companies Aug 2026 |
| IRS | Internal Revenue Service | The US federal tax agency |
| FinCEN | Financial Crimes Enforcement Network | US agency behind BOI/financial reporting rules |

Full definitions live in [glossary.md](glossary.md). Not tax advice — confirm with a qualified professional.
